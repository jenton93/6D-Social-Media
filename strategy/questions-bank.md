# Questions Bank

> **⛔ Empty — needs filling in. This is the highest-value 30 minutes in the
> whole plan.**

The 20 questions clients actually ask. In sales calls, in kick-off meetings,
in emails at 9pm, in the car park afterwards.

**In their words, not ours.** Not "What are the benefits of X" — but "Why did
the last one take four months?", "What happens if it fails?", "Can't we just
do this ourselves?", "Why are you twice the price of the other quote?"

This list becomes:
- 20 blog posts, one each, answered properly
- 40+ social posts (the answer cut down; the question on its own)
- The FAQ and objection-handling on every service page
- Most of what the directors post about

Nothing else produces content that sounds human as reliably, because none of
it is invented — it's transcribed.

Never close this file. Every time someone asks something, it goes in.
