# Profile Build-Out — Filling In The Pages Properly

**These matter more than they did.** The website carries no director
photographs or roles (client decision, 27 Aug), so LinkedIn is now where
anyone checking us out finds actual people. The Verify job leans on these
profiles rather than on the About page.

Right now the pages exist but aren't doing any work. A half-filled profile
costs us on every single post: someone reads a good post, clicks the name, and
lands on a page that tells them nothing. That's a lost enquiry, every time,
and it's the cheapest thing on this list to fix.

**Do this before publishing anything.** All three profiles complete, then
start the content calendar. Not in parallel.

## LinkedIn — 6D Group Ltd

| Field | Limit | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logo | 300×300px | ☐ | Must be legible at 48px — that's the size it appears at in feed |
| Cover image | 1128×191px | ☐ | Not a stock skyline. A line of positioning text or real work. Text stays in the middle third; the sides get cropped on mobile |
| Tagline | 120 chars | ☐ | The one that shows under the name everywhere. Say what we do and who for — not a slogan |
| About | 2,000 chars | ☐ | See structure below. Only the first ~250 chars show before "see more" |
| Website | — | ☐ | With UTM: `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=profile` |
| Industry | — | ☐ | Drives who LinkedIn shows us to. Get it right |
| Company size / HQ / Founded | — | ☐ | Blank fields read as dormant |
| Specialties | 20 max | ☐ | These are searchable. Use the words buyers type, not our internal names |
| Custom button | — | ☐ | "Visit website" → contact page |
| Featured / Products | — | ☐ | One entry per core service |
| Team association | — | ☐ | Every employee lists 6D Group Ltd as employer. Doubles organic reach and it's free |

**About section structure** (first 250 characters do the work):

```
[1] One sentence: what we do, who for, and the outcome.       ← visible before "see more"
[2] The problem our clients have before they call us.
[3] How we work — concrete, 3–4 sentences. Named process, not adjectives.
[4] Who we're a good fit for. And who we're not — this builds more trust
    than another paragraph of claims.
[5] Proof: numbers, sectors, years, named clients if permitted.
[6] What to do next, and what happens when they do.
```

## Consistency check

Once all three are filled in, verify across every channel:

- [ ] Same logo mark, same crop, same background
- [ ] Company name written identically everywhere (**6D Group Ltd**)
- [ ] The same one-sentence description of what we do — word for word
- [ ] Same primary link, each with its own UTM source
- [ ] Same contact email and phone
- [ ] Every page has at least one post from the last 30 days before we start
      driving traffic to it
- [ ] Every profile passes the voice check in `01-voice-and-tone.md` — profile
      copy is where "empowering businesses to unlock their potential" always
      creeps back in
