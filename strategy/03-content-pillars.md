# Content Pillars

Four pillars. Every piece belongs to exactly one, tagged in its front matter,
so the monthly diagnosis can compare output share against enquiry share.

> The pillar *names* below are the durable structure — they hold for almost
> any B2B service business. The *topics* inside them come straight from
> `00-brand-brief.md` and the questions bank, and can't be written until
> those exist.

---

### Pillar 1 — Answers (target ~40% of output)

The 20 questions clients actually ask, answered properly and without hedging.

- Highest search value, highest sales value, easiest to write well
- Every one of these is a blog post that a salesperson can send mid-deal
- Social cut: the question as the hook, the answer in 120 words

### Pillar 2 — Proof (~25%)

What we did, for whom, what happened. Numbers where we have them.

- Case studies, before/afters, "here's what we found when we looked"
- The pillar that converts. Weakest if we only publish the wins — one honest
  "this went wrong and here's what we changed" outperforms five polished ones
- Social cut: one detail from the job, one photo, one number

### Pillar 3 — Point of view (~25%)

What we think about how our industry works, and where it's wrong.

- Highest reach, highest risk, builds the recognition that makes Pillar 1 land
- Must be a position someone could argue with. If nobody could disagree, it
  isn't a point of view, it's a description
- Social cut: this pillar is native to LinkedIn — often the post *is* the piece

### Pillar 4 — People (~10%)

Who works here, how we work, what a week looks like.

- Lowest direct commercial return, highest effect on whether people trust us
- Native home is Instagram and Facebook
- Never staged. A genuinely mediocre photo of real work beats a good photo of
  a stock handshake

---

## Allocation, per month

At 1 blog + ~3 company-page social posts per week. Director posts
(`09-director-linkedin.md`) sit on top of this and draw from the same
pillars — weighted much more heavily toward Point of view:

| Pillar | Blogs (of ~4) | Social (of ~12) |
|---|---|---|
| Answers | 2 | 4 |
| Proof | 1 | 3 |
| Point of view | 1 | 4 |
| People | 0 | 1–2 |

Revisit this split every month against the enquiry data — §4 of
`06-analytics-framework.md`. If Proof is 25% of output and 60% of enquiries,
the split changes. That's the whole point of measuring.
