# Director Voice Note — Prompt Sheet

60–90 seconds, on your phone, no script. Pick whichever one you've actually
got an answer to this week.

1. What annoyed you at work this week?
2. What did someone ask you that you've now been asked more than twice?
3. What did a client say that stuck with you?
4. What nearly went wrong, and what did you change?
5. What does everyone in this industry believe that you think is wrong?
6. What surprised you in a number this month?
7. What would you tell someone about to make the mistake you made?

Don't tidy it up. Tangents, hesitations and "actually, no, the real reason
was…" are the parts that make the post sound like you. Those are kept.

You'll get a draft back that uses your words. Change anything that doesn't
sound like you, then it posts.
