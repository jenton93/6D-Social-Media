A CEO once told us he didn't want a countdown timer.

He also runs over. Every time.

So he needed a countdown timer. He just needed one that didn't feel like being
told off.

⛔ *Third example.*

I've lost count of how often this happens, and it isn't people being
difficult. They're thinking about the speech, or the room, or the thing
they've got to announce. Not about production.

Taking a brief literally is the easiest way to deliver exactly what someone
asked for and watch them be quietly disappointed by it.

Half this job is working out what's actually being asked.
