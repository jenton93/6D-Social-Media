## Permission status

Nothing is cleared. Track it here as replies come in.

### 1. Client permission, all fifteen

Logos on set, branded LED, sponsor walls, printed step-and-repeat. In every
frame a reader could name whose event it was, which under rule 6 in `CLAUDE.md`
is the same as writing the name in text.

**Clear the six, not the fifteen.** One email per client, naming the event and
attaching the frame. Ask for "the website and social media", not "marketing
use": the narrower ask gets the faster yes.

`photo-bankofchina*.jpg` and `photo-brandfinance.jpg` carry a third-party
photographer credit. **Being the production company on the job does not give us

`photo-brandfinance.jpg` shows a panel including a public figure whose presence

answer is we don't need it: it says nothing about our work that the other
fourteen don't. **Recommendation: leave it out and the question disappears.**

## The gap

All fifteen are the same moment in the job: the room, finished, before or during
the event. There is **no workshop, no build, no crew, no get-in, no cable, no
distro, no truss going up, nobody on a ladder.**

That matters because it's exactly what the copy claims hardest. The home page
argues that what you're buying is the people and how they're picked. The Set &
Staging page talks about an in-house workshop, CNC and stock panels. Neither has
a picture behind it.

**Two hours closes it, and needs nobody's permission:**

1. **One hour in the workshop.** Wide with the lights on, benches as they are, a
   print coming off, panels stacked, the CNC mid-cut. Don't tidy it. A tidied
   workshop looks like a showroom; a working one looks like a company
2. **Ten minutes mid-build on the next show.** Crew working, mid-distance, backs
   to camera, nobody posed

Both are unattributable. They can go up the day they're taken.

**And the free one:** the same room from one fixed spot, three times across a
day. Empty, mid-build, finished. Thirty seconds each, from a position someone is
standing in anyway. Nobody in this industry publishes it and it would explain
the whole business without a word of copy.

---

## The habit worth forming

Every one of these fifteen was shot after the branding went up. **The same
rooms, photographed an hour earlier or from three feet to the side, would have
been publishable the day they were taken with nobody's permission needed.**

From now on, shoot everything twice: once with the branding in frame and once
without. The clean twin costs nothing and it's the difference between a photo
library you can use and one you have to negotiate for.
