## Then read it aloud

Every page, out loud, before it goes over. That's the test in
`strategy/01-voice-and-tone.md` §6 and it catches what silent reading doesn't.
Anything you wouldn't say to a producer in the van, change it, that instinct
is more reliable than any style guide.
